Popular Malaysian Destinations

Discover lively cities, peaceful islands, historic towns and beautiful highland scenery across Malaysia.

Kuala Lumpur

Federal Territory

Malaysia's capital is known for the Petronas Twin Towers, lively markets and shopping.

Langkawi

Kedah

A beautiful island destination with sandy beaches, clear water and sunsets.

Penang

Penang

Famous for George Town heritage buildings, street art and local food.

Malacca

Melaka

Explore historic architecture, museums and colourful Jonker Street.

Genting Highlands

Pahang

A cool mountain resort with scenic views and entertainment.

Cameron Highlands

Pahang

Known for tea plantations, strawberry farms and nature walks.

Sabah

Malaysian Borneo

Offers rainforest adventures, wildlife and Mount Kinabalu views.

Perhentian Islands

Terengganu

Popular for snorkelling, diving and tropical beach holidays.
